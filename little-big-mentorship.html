LITTLE BIG MENTORSHIP

1-2 semester long mentorship with industry pros and alumni

Program Info

The Little Big Mentorship program is an opportunity for paid members to either be a mentor and teach others under their preferred discipline, or be a mentee and learn a skill of their choosing. Mentorship is one of the most invaluable ways to get tailored career guidance, gain advice/knowledge on portfolio building, develop technical skills, create meaningful projects, and build a long-term professional relationship. Students looking to break into the industry are highly encouraged to participate.

Note: You must be a dues-paying member to be eligible. See more information on how to pay dues here.

Some of the skills you can learn or teach are:

  • Programming
  • 3D Art
  • 2D Art
  • Tech Art/Animation
  • Level Design
  • Tech Design
  • Production
  • Audio
  • Generalist/Undecided
