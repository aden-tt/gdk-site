What is the Game Developers Conference (GDC)

Game Developers Conference (GDC) is one of the largest professional conferences for the video game industry. Hosted every year in San Francisco, it brings together game developers, artists, programmers, designers, students, educators, and other industry professionals from around the world.

Throughout the conference, attendees can participate in talks and panels, explore industry showcases, learn about new technologies and development tools, attend career events, and connect with other people working in the game industry.

For students, GDC is an opportunity to learn directly from industry professionals, explore different careers in game development, build professional connections, and experience one of the industry's largest events firsthand. For those preparing for internships and full-time positions, GDC can also be a valuable opportunity to practice introducing yourself, discussing your work, networking professionally, and learning what studios look for in potential candidates.

What the trip will cover:

The cost for the trip will cover the following:

  • GDC Badge (Academic Pass)
  • Hotel accomodations
  • Round-trip airfare to San Francisco

This means we would purchase the badge, hotel, and airfare for you. Students will be responsible for any other personal expenses, including food, local transportation, and other activities or purchases during the trip.

GDC Academic Pass

For GDC 2027, the Academic Pass is essentially the full GDC experience at a reduced price for students. It includes:

  • All conference sessions and talks
  • Festival Hall access Tuesday–Thursday
  • GDC Nights, including the Developer’s Concert, Game Developers Choice Awards, and Independent Games Festival Awards
  • Friday Career Faire and career-focused sessions
  • Business Hall access

Note: It does not include GDC Vault access.

Cost

The trip is expected to cost approximately $600–$700 per student. This estimate is based on current projected expenses and is subject to change if necessary. If the cost changes, students will be notified as soon as possible.

Students will pay via a payment link and will have two options:

  • Pay in 3 installments according to the provided payment deadlines
  • Pay the full amount at once

For example, last year's payment schedule was $150-250 payments split across three months.

The exact payment amounts and deadlines for GDC 2027 will be provided to selected students.

Missed Payments

Students must submit each payment by its stated deadline. If a student does not pay the required amount by the deadline, they may be removed from the trip and their spot given to another student on the waitlist.

Any students selected from the waitlist will be given additional time to submit their payments.

Eligibility

This trip is open to current UCF students interested in game development and related fields. Space is limited to 20 students.

To be eligible, students must:

  • Be enrolled at UCF during both Fall 2026 and Spring 2027.
  • Be a dues-paying member of Game Dev Knights for the 2026–2027 academic year or Spring 2027 semester.
  • All majors are allowed

How are students selected to go?

Priority will first be given to students who completed our GDC 2027 Interest Form earlier this summer.

If additional spots become available, students may join a waitlist. Students on the waitlist will then be prioritized based on class standing, with seniors receiving priority.
