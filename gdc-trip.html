Come with us to Game Developers Conference!

The UCF Game Development Knights are organizing a group trip to the 2027 Game Developers Conference (GDC) in San Francisco, California.

GDC is one of the largest professional conferences for the game development industry, featuring talks, panels, workshops, networking opportunities, career events, and an expo featuring tools and technologies used throughout the industry.

This is an opportunity for students to attend GDC as a group at a reduced rate.

What is the Game Developers Conference (GDC)

Game Developers Conference (GDC) is one of the largest professional conferences for the video game. Hosted every year in San Francisco, it brings together game developers, artists, programmers, designers, students, educators, and other industry professionals from around the world.

Throughout the conference, attendees can participate in talks and panels, explore industry showcases, learn about new technologies and development tools, attend career events, and connect with other people working in the game industry.

For students, GDC is an opportunity to learn directly from industry professionals, explore different careers in game development, build professional connections, and experience one of the industry's largest events firsthand.

For those preparing for internships and full-time positions, GDC can also be a valuable opportunity to practice introducing yourself, discussing your work, networking professionally, and learning what studios look for in potential candidates.

Trip Information

  • Conference: Game Developers Conference 2027
  • Location: Moscone Center, San Francisco, California
  • Conference Dates: March 1 - 5
  • Travel Dates: February 28 - March 6
  • Cost: estimated $600-700*

*Costs subject to change.

Cost

The trip is expected to cost approximately $600–$700 per student. This estimate is based on current projected expenses and is subject to change if necessary. If the cost changes, students will be notified as soon as possible.

Students will pay via a payment link and will have two options:

  • Pay in 3 installments according to the provided payment deadlines
  • Pay the full amount at once

For example, last year's payment schedule was approximately: First payment: $150 due late October. Second payment: $250 due in November. Third payment: $250 due in December

The exact payment amounts and deadlines for GDC 2027 will be provided to selected students.

Missed Payments

Students must submit each payment by its stated deadline. If a student does not pay the required amount by the deadline, they may be removed from the trip and their spot given to another student on the waitlist.

Any students selected from the waitlist will be given additional time to submit their payments.

The trip cost will cover:

  • GDC Badge (Academic Pass)
  • Hotel accomodations
  • Round-trip airfare to San Francisco

Students will be responsible for any other personal expenses, including food, local transportation, and other activities or purchases during the trip.

GDC Academic Pass

For GDC 2027, the Academic Pass is essentially the full GDC experience at a reduced price for students. It includes:

  • All conference sessions and talks
  • Festival Hall access Tuesday–Thursday
  • GDC Nights, including the Developer’s Concert, Game Developers Choice Awards, and Independent Games Festival Awards
  • Friday Career Faire and career-focused sessions
  • Business Hall access

Note: It does not include GDC Vault access.

Who Can Apply?

This trip is open to current UCF students interested in game development and related fields. Space is limited to 20 students.

To be eligible, students must be:

  • Be enrolled at UCF during both Fall 2026 and Spring 2027.
  • Be a dues-paying member of Game Dev Knights for the 2026–2027 academic year or Spring 2027 semester.
  • All majors are allowed

How to Apply

1. Complete the GDC Interest Form

During the summer, students were able to submit a GDC 2027 Interest Form to indicate that they were interested in attending the conference. Students who completed this interest form will receive priority consideration for the trip.

The interest form for GDC 2027 is now closed.


2. Complete the Official GDC Trip Application

All students who wish to attend the trip must also complete the official GDC 2027 Registration & Waitlist Form, which will be released early October.

Important: Completing the summer interest form does not automatically register you for the trip. Students who previously completed the interest form must submit the official application as well.

Students who completed the summer interest form will still receive priority when applications are reviewed.

If additional spots become available, students may join a waitlist. Students on the waitlist will then be prioritized based on class standing, with seniors receiving priority.


3. Confirm Your Spot

If you are selected to go, you will be contacted by a GDK officer to confirm your spot.

Note: Space on the trip is limited. Completing these forms do not guarantee a spot on the trip. Students will be contacted with additional information regarding selection, funding, travel arrangements, and required documentation.

Full Process

  1. Apply: Complete the official GDC 2027 trip application.
  2. Confirm your spot: Students will be contacted to confirm their interest in going to GDC
  3. Pay the trip fee: Students selected to attend will be responsible for paying their required portion of the trip cost.
  4. Receive GDC pass and boarding pass: Students will receive their GDC badges and flight information shortly before the trip.
  5. Travel to San Francisco: We will travel together as a group, pick up our GDC badges, and check into the hotel.
  6. Attend GDC! Once we get checked into our accommodations, students will have the freedom to explore the conference and San Francisco on their own. We will come back together as a group at the end of the trip for our return travel.

How are students selected to go?

Priority will first be given to students who completed our GDC 2027 Interest Form earlier this summer.

If additional spots become available, students may join a waitlist. Students on the waitlist will then be prioritized based on class standing, with seniors receiving priority.
