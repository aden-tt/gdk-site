WELCOME TO GDK!

University of Central Florida's official game development club

GameDev Knights (GDK) is UCF's official game development club. We are a community of students passionate about creating games. We host regular meetings at the downtown campus, alongside hands-on workshops, game jams, team projects, mentorship programs, and networking events.

OUR PROGRAMS

GAME JAMS

Get paired with seasoned game industry professionals and alumni for a semester of one-on-one guidance, portfolio reviews, and career advice.

PROJECT FORGE

Collaborate in teams to build a game from scratch around a surprise theme within a tight timeframe. Perfect for rapid prototyping, learning new tools, and shipping a complete project. No prior experience needed.

LITTLE BIG MENTORSHIP PROGRAM

Get paired with seasoned game industry professionals and alumni for a semester of one-on-one guidance, portfolio reviews, and career advice.

GAME DEVELOPERS CONFERENCE

Travel with the club to the Game Developers Conference (GDC) in San Francisco to network with industry professionals, attend career panels, and explore the expo floor. Applications open every fall.

SPRING CAREER FAIR

Connect directly with recruiters and studio heads from companies looking for programmers, artists, and designers. Learn more about local oppurtunities, practice your pitch, share your portfolio, and land internships or full-time roles.
