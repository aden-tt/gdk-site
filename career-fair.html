Downtown CAREER FAIR

Spring career fair for games, simulation, and interactive media

Overview

The Downtown UCF GaIM Career Fair is an annual event hosted by GDK in partnership with Downtown UCF Career Services and the Digital Media Program with the goal to allow local companies in industry and relevant post-secondary educational programs to table and connect with students about resources and internship/employment opportunities.

In our first career fair in the spring of 2026, we had 100+ students attending and 7 employers/organizations tabling.

The next Downtown UCF GaIM Career Fair is planned for early-April 2027.

Interested in tabling?

If you are a company or organization who is interested in tabling at the Downtown Career Fair, please reach out at gamedevknights@gmail.com and an officer will be in contact within 24-72 hours.
