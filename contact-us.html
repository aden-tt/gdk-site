Location / Directions

Our club primarily operates on the UCF Downtown Campus because that is where the Game Design program is located. Our events often take place in the Communication and Media Building, which is right next to Parramore Garage.

We recommend parking at Parramore Garage or Amelia Garage. Students can park in either garage using their standard commuter "D" permits. Otherwise, you can purchase a daily virtual permit for $8.

Not a Downtown student / need transportation?

Not a problem! UCF provides free shuttles that go back and forth between main campus and downtown campus. All you need is your UCFID to board. See more information aobut the UCF Downtown shuttle here.
